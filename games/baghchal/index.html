Bagh-Chal Rules

Objective: An asymmetric traditional board game from Nepal. Tigers try to capture 5 goats. Goats try to completely trap all 4 tigers.

Phase 1: Goat Placement

Goats start off the board. On each turn, the Goat player places 1 goat onto any vacant intersection point on the 5x5 grid.

Phase 2: Goat Movement

Once all 20 goats are placed, Goats can move 1 step along connected lines to adjacent empty intersections.

Tiger Moves & Captures

4 Tigers start at the four corners. Tigers can move 1 step to an adjacent empty spot OR jump over a goat into an empty spot directly beyond it to capture (kill) it.

Win Conditions

• Tigers Win: Capture 5 Goats.
• Goats Win: Block all 4 Tigers so none can make any legal move.
