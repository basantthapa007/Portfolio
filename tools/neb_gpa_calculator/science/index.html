How NEB Science Stream GPA Calculation Works

In the NEB Class 11 and 12 Science curriculum established by the Curriculum Development Centre (CDC) Nepal, total credit hours are distributed into theory (75%) and practical (25%) components:

  • Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology, Computer Science: 5 Credit Hours each (3.75 Theory / 1.25 Practical)
  • English: 4 Credit Hours (3.0 Theory / 1.0 Practical)
  • Nepali: 3 Credit Hours (2.25 Theory / 0.75 Practical)

NEB Passing Rule: Students must score a minimum grade of D (35% or 1.6 GP) in theory examinations. Scoring below 35% in any subject's theory paper results in a Non-Graded (NG) status.
