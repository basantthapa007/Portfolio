⌄ Calculation process:

To calculate your NEB GPA, you must first convert your subject percentages into Grade Points (on a 4.0 scale) and identify the Credit Hours assigned to each subject. For every subject, multiply its Grade Point by its Credit Hours to find that subject's total "quality points." Finally, add all these quality points together and divide that total sum by your total credit hours (which usually equals 27) to get your final GPA.

Keep in mind that NEB calculates theory and practical grades separately, and you must score at least 35% in theory to pass. If you score below 35% in the theory section of any subject, you will receive an NG (Not Graded), which temporarily blocks your final GPA calculation until you pass the grade increment makeup exam.:
