⌄ How NEB GPA Calculation Works:

To calculate your National Examination Board (NEB) GPA, convert your subject percentages into Grade Points (on a 4.0 scale) and identify the Credit Hours assigned to each subject. Multiply each subject's Grade Point by its Credit Hours to get total quality points, then divide the sum by your total credit hours (typically 27).

Note that NEB calculates theory and practical grades separately. You must score at least 35% in theory to pass. Scoring below 35% in any theory section results in an NG (Not Graded), temporarily blocking your final GPA until you clear the grade increment makeup exam.
